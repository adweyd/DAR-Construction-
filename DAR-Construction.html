About DAR

Building is a promise kept in concrete, steel and time.

DAR Construction was built on a simple idea: a drawing is only as good as the building it becomes. We work as both architects and builders, so the plan that gets approved is the plan that gets constructed — no gap between the design table and the site.

Based in Kazhakuttom, we take on residential and commercial projects across Thiruvananthapuram, staying involved at every stage: design, approvals, materials, site work and final handover.

How a project runs

From first sketch to handed-over keys.

Four stages, one team responsible for all of them.

01

Consult & design

We visit the site, understand your brief and budget, and prepare drawings and a 3D view of the proposed building.

02

Approvals & planning

Structural drawings, permit documentation and a detailed cost and material plan are finalised before any work begins.

03

Construction

Our crew handles foundation, structure and finishing on site, with a supervisor tracking progress against the schedule.

04

Handover

Final inspection, snag-list closure and documentation, then the keys — and the building — are handed over to you.
